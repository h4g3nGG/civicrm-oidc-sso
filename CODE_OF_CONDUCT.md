# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We aim to maintain a respectful, inclusive and technically rigorous project.
Contributors must communicate constructively, welcome well-founded questions,
respect differing experience levels and focus criticism on ideas and code.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of
sensitive information and personal attacks are not acceptable. Security
reports must be handled privately and responsibly.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in the repository, issue tracker, reviews and other public
or private project communication. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate
content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
necessary to protect contributors or the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to
`civicrm-oidc-sso@whatchamacallit.xyz`. Include only the information necessary
to investigate the concern. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and
confidentially as reasonably possible.
